- **PRD:** `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/festgrid-prd-2026-07-10-2047/prd.md` (updated 2026-10-21, commit `ea81cb3`)
- **Rubric:** `.claude/skills/bmad-prd/assets/prd-validation-checklist.md`
- **Scope:** only what the 2026-10-21 amendment added or changed: new §3.18 to §3.21 (FR116 to FR125), new §4.21, FR126 in §3.9.3, and the amendments inside §3.1, §3.3, §3.7, §3.9.3, §3.15, §4.1, §4.3, §4.4, §4.5, §4.7, §4.18 and §7. Older content was read only to check consistency with the amendment and is not graded. Line numbers below are lines of the current `prd.md`.
- **Checked against:** `source-extract-dedup-grouping.md`, `source-extract-location-audience-moderator.md`, `.memlog.md` (last entries) and the spine's AD-14 Rule 4 (e) and AD-39, to see whether adopted items were carried faithfully.
- **Derived grade (validate.md rule):** **Fair** (three high findings, two thin dimensions, no critical finding).
- **Counts:** critical 0, high 3, medium 11, low 6.

## Overall verdict

The amendment is the best-evidenced content in the PRD: nearly every rule carries a production number or a named failure behind it ("0 of 147 suggested matches", "336 of 432 pins", "a different venue in each of 12 posts"), costs are named where the authors saw them ("The cost is accepted", "Published, Not Held"), unproven items are tagged, and adopted decisions from the spine were carried over faithfully. What holds it back is that three load-bearing terms are not pinned down: "the viewer's area", the equality tests that decide when events are collapsed or merged, and which "country" the grouping and merge guards use. Each of these can hide or merge a real event in front of a user, so they need closing before story creation. The remaining gaps are mostly bookkeeping (no Assumptions Index, inconsistent "planned" markers, half-amended §3.15 text).

## Decision-readiness — adequate

Where the authors saw a tension they named it with its price: §3.19 "Same Country Only" ("The cost is accepted: an Indonesian event whose only location is a wrong foreign pin ... stays ungrouped"), §3.20 "Unknown Area Is Published, Not Held" (the rejected alternative and why: "a hold would be indefinite and silent"), §3.18 "Delivery" (sweep first, live check off until the owner has read the result). No `[NOTE FOR PM]` callout is used anywhere in the amendment, and three real tensions are carried as evidence rather than as decisions.

### Findings
- **[medium]** The "moderators do not work queues" thesis is not followed through for cross-poster duplicates and group review (§3.18 line 345, §3.19 lines 364 to 365, §3.9.3 lines 200 to 201). The PRD argues three times that a moderator step cannot be the mechanism ("0 of 147 suggested matches resolved"). Yet the cross-poster duplicate class (source extract §8 P2: 7 of 24 cross-account clusters) is left to "Cross-Post Event Matching", which is that queue, and the fix is only "`[ASSUMPTION]` ... proposed, not decided". The review path for groups and the nightly cross-account list both depend on FR126, which itself "depends on e-mail and push delivery working in the deployed environment ... (BUG-073)". The §7 counter-metric "duplicate pairs still visible per 1,000 open events" therefore cannot fall for this class until FR126 works. *Fix:* state it as a named decision with a `[NOTE FOR PM]`: either accept residual cross-poster duplicates until FR126 ships, or promote the cross-poster rule to an adopted requirement.
- **[medium]** The price of the verified-pin rules is given as evidence, not as a trade-off (§3.20 lines 371 and 374, §3.7 line 86). "336 of 432 pins outside Indonesia name no country ... and would be withheld, as all 42 pins labelled by hand were wrong" reads as if the 336 are known wrong; only the 42 sample are. The spine states the cost plainly ("for any genuine foreign event whose text does not say where it is"), and the PRD omits it, unlike §3.19. Separately, §3.20 lets a venue account's default location produce a verified pin (map link, Nearby), while §3.7 line 86 lets that default be an AI inference applied at a confidence of only `0.5`. The PRD never says the default must be human-set or moderator-accepted before it can verify a pin. *Fix:* add one sentence naming the accepted cost (genuine foreign events whose text names no country lose their map link) and state whether an AI-inferred default may verify a pin.

## Substance over theater — strong

No persona, innovation or NFR theater. Thresholds are concrete and product-specific (identical date range plus start time for an exact repeat, first live run limited to 20 clusters, 120/30-day series gaps, 90 s notification delay, 7-day suppression, 0.5 pin floor), and the Vision-level claims are replaced by measured failures.

### Findings
- **[low]** Point-in-time production statistics sit inside normative text (§3.18 line 342, §3.20 lines 371, 374 and 376, §3.21 line 384, §3.9.3 line 200: "0 of 147", "336 of 432", "34% of the open events ... 22%", "when this was decided"). They are valuable rationale but will be stale within weeks and are mixed with the rule itself. *Fix:* move them into a short "Evidence at time of decision" note per section, so the requirement sentence stays timeless.

## Strategic coherence — adequate

The thesis is present and consistent: automatic first, reversible, never gated on a moderator, evidence before build (FR116, FR117, FR123, FR126, "Not Yet Proven" in §3.21). Prioritisation follows it (sweep before live check, groups apply immediately, curator area never blocks a scrape). It is never stated once, and measurement covers only part of the amendment.

### Findings
- **[medium]** §7 measures dedup and grouping but not the place model or the moderator queue (§7 line 1844, "FR116-FR119" only). FR121 to FR125 have no metric: share of open events by `EXACT`/`AREA`/`UNKNOWN`, pins withheld by the country test, unknown-area events resolved by the nightly job, audience-country coverage of new events. FR126 has only "suggested matches waiting longer than 7 days". The counter-metric "events hidden by a wrong group" cannot be measured as worded; "share dissolved or detached by a moderator" is its only proxy, and that proxy depends on the queue being worked. *Fix:* add one bullet per missing area, and reword the hidden-events counter-metric to what is actually countable.
- **[low]** The unifying principle is implicit and §2 Goals is untouched. A two-sentence statement ("automatic-first, reversible, evidence-led; no display or ingestion step waits on a moderator") at the top of §3.18 would let a reader check each later rule against it, and linking the amendment to the Comprehensive Event Coverage and Engagement goals would say why it matters.

## Done-ness clarity — thin

Several FRs carry exactly the testable consequences downstream story creation needs (§3.18: "the date range must be identical, not merely overlapping"; §3.18 undo "records 'not a duplicate'"; §3.19 "one notification ... per group and source post"). The thin part is the predicates that decide whether a user sees an event: they are adjectives or undefined inputs.

### Findings
- **[high]** "In your area" and the local ordering have no defined input (§3.1 line 37, §3.3 line 52, §3.20 line 373). The label "marks an event whose area is the viewer's area or inside it", but the PRD never says what the viewer's area is. Saved locations (§3.3) are named points with a radius, browser location is optional (§3.1 Nearby), and users have no country or area field. The "no exact location" lane (§3.3, §3.20) has no membership rule either: which area-only events appear for a 5 km radius around a saved point? The source extract flagged this as Q4 under "Open questions that block a requirement" and the PRD carries it as neither an `[ASSUMPTION]` nor a `[NOTE FOR PM]`. *Fix:* define the viewer's area (saved location, else current location, else none, and the resolution from point to area keys, non-persisted) and the lane membership rule, or tag the gap as an open item.
- **[high]** The tests that collapse or merge events rest on adjectives and undefined terms (§3.19 lines 356 and 359, §3.18 lines 341 and 345, §3.20 lines 374 to 376). "when the promo name is near-identical" and "It collapses only where the occurrences carry the same promo name" decide which events vanish behind a cover card; the spine has a weighted name bar (0.85, itself an assumption) that the PRD dropped without tagging. "same name, same place" has no normalisation or place-equality definition. "same poster" (§3.18 lines 341, 345) is not tied to the publisher/co-author roles of §4.7a, so it is unclear whether a co-author repost counts (source extract Q6 asked for one definition per rule). "the narrowest area that contains most of the first posts" (§3.20 line 375) gives no count for "first" or "most" (spine N=5, M=3, assumptions, also dropped). "no usable place text" (§3.20 line 376) is undefined. "a confirmed organizer/venue/event account of local or regional reach" (§3.20 line 374) names an attribute that exists nowhere in §4.5. *Fix:* state each predicate as a bounded test, carrying the spine start values as tagged `[ASSUMPTION]` where they are not final; add the `reach` attribute and its source to §4.5 or say how it is read.
- **[medium]** FR125 has no consumer, no coverage bound and a dangling source (§3.21 lines 380 to 384, §4.1 lines 472 to 480). The headline "Every Event Has an Audience Country" is not testable beside "Existing events stay without one" and the optional `audienceCountry?`. Step (3) of the source order reads "the account's own country when it has one", but §4.5 has no such field. Step (4) "the platform's default country" is never named (Indonesia / `id` appears only in the spine). The only stated behaviour is negative ("never feeds grouping or the check on a pin's country"), and "How the value orders or filters Discovery is not decided here". The side effect is that an online or nationwide event with no place has an unknown area and therefore takes the FR123 penalty and per-page limit, which §3.21 line 382 accepts ("it keeps its lower rank and limit ... because its area is unknown"). That outcome defeats the purpose of the field and is not named as a trade-off. *Fix:* state a coverage target for newly ingested events, name the default country, add or drop the account-country input, and say whether online and nationwide events are intended to take the unknown-area penalty.
- **[medium]** Where collapse and lanes apply is only half listed (§3.19 line 359, §3.1 lines 38 to 40, §3.15 line 298, §3.14). The PRD names "the calendar, favorites, the subscribed feed and account pages" as always listing every event. It is silent on the Embeddable Widget, "My AI Filters" results, the post collection page, Archive, and on how the "closest matches" and "no exact location" lanes behave under a boolean expression: `NOT` and `OR` over `areaKey` with lane semantics are undefined, and the widget uses "the identical shared compiler". "Lane" itself is never defined (separate section, same page, paginated or not). *Fix:* list the surfaces where collapse and lanes apply and state that lanes exist only for a plain area filter, or define them inside expressions.
- **[medium]** FR119's two suppression rules collide for the headline case, a weekly series from one account (§3.19 line 361). Rule 1 notifies "once for those members" when a later post adds members; rule 2 says a user "is not notified about it more than once within 7 days". A weekly post lands about 7 days after the last, so the outcome flips on the boundary, and the PRD does not say whether the suppressed notification is dropped or deferred. Source extract Q1 raised this conflict; the PRD resolved only the multi-account half. *Fix:* state which rule wins for a single account, whether the window is inclusive, and what happens to the suppressed one.
- **[medium]** Area derivation has a hole for place text whose pin fails verification (§3.20 lines 369, 372 and 374). §3.20 says "None is inferred from another", takes an area from "a verified pin" only, and says "A stated place always wins over any fallback". An event with place text, an unverified pin and no stated area therefore has a stated place but no area, and then falls to the account fallback, which can place it in the account's default area, contradicting the place it states. *Fix:* say whether place text with an unverified pin counts as "a stated place" for the fallback, and what area it gets.
- **[medium]** FR126 has no acceptance bound and the "pending" semantics are open (§3.9.3 lines 200 to 201). "Moderators are told when items are waiting" has no time bound; cadence, recipients, opt-out and failure display are deferred under one `[ASSUMPTION]`. The badge now counts "Group suggestions" and "curator accounts without an audience area", but groups apply immediately and a curator area may stay unresolved indefinitely (§3.20 line 375: "when neither yields an area it stays unresolved"). Nothing says what removes such an item from the count, so it can stay permanently high. *Fix:* define the resolved state for group-review and no-area items, and give FR126 one testable bound (e.g. a digest sent within N hours of an item first waiting).
- **[medium]** §3.15 and §4.18 are half-amended (§3.15 lines 296, 300 and 301, §4.18 lines 1611, 1683 and 1624). Line 301 now says region matching uses the event's area, but line 296 still lists "admin-area/region text match" as the prompt vocabulary and line 300 still says venue type is stored "alongside the new admin-area field". Line 301 itself keeps its first three sentences ("relies on the existing geolocation provider's administrative-area data ... community-sourced (OpenStreetMap)") ahead of the amendment that reverses them. Nothing says how a prompt phrase ("Sleman") becomes a gazetteer `areaKey`, and §4.18 line 1683 still says "nothing that predates this amendment is migrated or behaves differently" while the meaning of region filtering has changed for previously saved AI filters and widgets. *Fix:* rewrite lines 296, 300 and 301 to the area model, state which component resolves a region phrase to a key, and say what happens to saved filters that carry `adminArea` text.
- **[low]** Event-level precision and schedule-level pin trust can disagree (§4.1 line 463 versus §4.4 line 729). `locationPrecision` is `EXACT` "when the main schedule's pin is verified", but `pinTrust` is documented as the only thing the map link, radius filter and Nearby badge read, per schedule. An event with an untrusted main schedule and a trusted second schedule would be `AREA` yet match a radius. *Fix:* state which schedule the radius filter and Nearby badge evaluate.

## Scope honesty — adequate

Honest in the main: "(planned)" markers on the place split and lineup reshape, "Not Yet Proven" on FR125, `[ASSUMPTION]` on start values, a live check that ships off, and explicit "not covered" statements ("Different posters announcing the same event at the same place are not covered by the exact-repeat rule"). The inconsistency is in how consistently delivery status is shown.

### Findings
- **[medium]** Delivery status is marked for some requirements and not others, and two schema fields carry proposals with no prose (§3.19 lines 354 to 365, §4.21 lines 1455 to 1476, §3.18 line 341). §4.21 says "Planned; not yet in production" while §3.19 and §3.1 line 40 are written in the present tense; §3.18's headline "FestGrid shows it once" does not carry the condition that the live check ships off. `EventGroup.placeCount` and `EventGroup.concurrent` ("also at N other places") have no behaviour in §3.19 and are PROPOSED in the spine (source extract §8 P8), yet carry no tag. *Fix:* mark each FR as shipped, planned or behind a switch, and either describe or tag the two fields.
- **[medium]** The planned re-extraction omits an adopted constraint (§3.19 line 363). "existing upcoming events are planned to be re-extracted the same way" omits the adopted rule that re-extraction uses only the Gemini keys of contributors who subscribe to the account that posted the event, and that an event with no such subscriber stays unsplit (source extract A11). In a BYOK-only product (§3.7, §3.8) this decides whose quota is spent. It also meets the guarantees "No Duplicate Re-Extraction" and "a field that has an approved user correction or a moderator edit is never overwritten" (§3.7) with no stated resolution. *Fix:* add the key rule and the interaction with corrections and soft-deleted events.
- **[low]** Open items the author logged were not carried into the PRD. `.memlog.md` lists, to triage in Finalize, that the Primary Post clause (2) "the post whose extraction gave more schedules" (§3.7 line 104) is an `[ASSUMPTION]` in the spine but stated as fact, and that Timezone Inference (§3.7 line 138, "The event's location will be used") now sits beside a pin-verification rule that does not say whether an unverified pin may feed it. Neither is visible to a PRD reader. Also, "A programme is never collapsed and never capped" (§3.19 line 359) is stronger than the adopted position, which keeps an optional soft cap behind a flag, off by default (source extract A27). *Fix:* tag both items in the PRD and reword the programme sentence to "not capped by default".

## Downstream usability — thin

IDs and cross-references hold up (see Mechanical notes), and every new schema field cites its FR and section, which is good traceability. The problem is vocabulary: one phrase, "aimed at ... country", is used for three different facts.

### Findings
- **[high]** Three country facts share one phrase, and only one has a source order or a field (§3.18 line 343, §3.19 line 357, §3.20 line 371, §3.21 lines 380 to 382, §4.1 lines 472 to 480). §3.21 defines "the country it is aimed at" as the audience country. Yet §3.18 ("events aimed at different countries" are never merged) and §3.19 ("Events aimed at different countries are never grouped") use it for the guard, while §3.21 line 382 says the guards use "the country an event takes place in", a different fact: "Section 3.19 uses the latter, strictly". That place country has no source order, no field in §4.1 and no rule for "cannot be settled"; and a third notion, the pin check's "expected country" (§3.20 line 371), takes the single country named in the poster's text or the platform default. An implementer who follows §3.21's definition would use `audienceCountry`, including its `default` source, which §3.21 forbids; the sweep also cannot test an audience country on existing events, which "stay without one". *Fix:* name the three facts differently (for example audience country, place country, expected country for pins), give the place country its source order and storage in §4.1, and make §3.18 and §3.19 say which one they use.
- **[low]** FR traceability is coarse (§3.20, §3.1, FR121). FR121 is cited in 13 places across §3.1, §3.3, §3.15, §3.20, §4.1, §4.3 and §4.18 for five distinct behaviours (badge, area filter, radius lane, three-facts model, region matching), so one story cannot close it and one test cannot trace to it. `epics.md` does not hold FR116 to FR126 yet (its inventory stops at FR115). *Fix:* split FR121 by behaviour and add the FR116 to FR126 inventory to `epics.md`, as earlier amendments did.

## Shape fit — adequate

FestDaily is a consumer product with several protagonists; the amendment is brownfield and mostly capability and contract text, which suits it, and it separates today's behaviour from planned behaviour better than most of the older sections ("today only five event types are e-mailed to moderators", "nothing in production wrote this field").

### Findings
- **[low]** The two rules with the most user-visible edge cases have no walk-through (FR119 notification suppression, FR121 "In your area" and lanes). The PRD has no user journeys anywhere, which is a pre-existing choice, but a three-beat scene ("a user follows two mall accounts that post one promo", "a Sleman user opens Discovery") would have caught the weekly-series collision and the undefined viewer's area noted above.
