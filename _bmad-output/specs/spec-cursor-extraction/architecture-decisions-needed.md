# Architecture decisions needed

For `bmad-architecture` to follow. The highest existing decision in `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/festgrid-architecture-spine.md` is AD-45, so new numbers start at AD-46 (proposed; re-check before writing). Read the existing sections with `python3 scripts/md-outline.py find "^AD-N:" <file>`.

| Proposed | Decision | Why it needs a decision | Existing text it touches |
|---|---|---|---|
| AD-46 | Multi-step (cursor) extraction for selected posts | Reverses AD-13 Rule 1 ("one request per post, always") and Rule 3 (loop rejected, revisit if the cap proves insufficient). Defines which posts leave the one-request path (`continue-rules.md`) and records the quota trade-off. Amends AD-30 Rule 5 ("one Gemini call per post"; the per-post event cap of 10 is lifted on the cursor path, a per-call cap of about 25 remains). | AD-13, AD-30 Rule 5 |
| AD-47 | Step message contract, in-progress state and automatic resume | Cursor in the queue message and persisted on the post (nullable `posts` column; null when no chain is in flight); one audit row per step (readers of "the latest audit row" must be reviewed); `posts.is_extracted` and the extraction claim stay in progress until the last step; resume is automatic: a resume pass at the start of the AI Lambda's 5-minute tick re-enqueues the next step of posts with a cursor, a stale claim, valid images and attempts left (attempts counted in the cursor, growing delay); a post whose images expire or attempts run out has its cursor cleared and is flagged partial permanently. Before giving up on expired images the resume pass re-scrapes for fresh links and keeps the cursor (see AD-51). A dead-lettered copy is harmless because steps are idempotent. | AD-29 audit table, Story 3.6z claim TTL, the 5-minute poll-and-drain schedule |
| AD-48 | Extraction ordinals for incremental steps | `(post, extraction_ordinal)` is unique, idempotent (AD-30 Rule 3), `smallint`, and drives the slug suffix, but ordinals are assigned once, globally, by earliest date. Options: (a) per-step ranges from a next-free ordinal carried in the cursor (precedent: AD-30 Rule 8 "next free ordinal, max + 1"; `assignApplyOrdinals` and `PARKED_ORDINAL_BASE` in the re-extract Apply); (b) content-key idempotency (normalized name + start date) replacing the ordinal. Also the cross-step dedupe key. | AD-30 Rules 1, 3, 8; AD-40 Apply |
| AD-49 | Gemini client contract: truncation and usage signals | `finishReason` and `candidatesTokenCount` are exposed; truncation and at-risk rules; a parse failure is retried or recorded, not dropped; whether the response schema can carry the post plan without a Gemini 400. | `gemini-client.ts`, AD-30 Rule 5 payload, AD-29 |
| AD-50 | Partial posts and the roundup UX read model | Where "partial" lives: a pending cursor means "still being finished" and a permanent `posts.extraction_partial` flag (images expired or attempts spent) means "will not be finished"; the page note shows for either; the sibling list as a read that adds no join to `Query.events` / `Query.eventBySlug` (AD-30 Rule 4; precedent `Query.relatedEventIds`). | AD-30 Rules 4 and 10 |
| AD-51 | Request bound, kill switch and provider-error policy | The bound by construction (requests per post at most twice the unread slides) and its invariant check; the kill-switch env name; 429 and 503 handling (queue retry with a delay, serial steps per post, inter-step delay); per-step blur and geocoding budgets (about one geocode call per schedule). Re-scrape policy for expired images: at most 2 per post, 10 minutes apart, through AD-32, the AD-34 gate and the FIND-110 lease; a refused or failed scrape (credit-limited vendor) ends the post partial; only the cursor's image links change; the slide count must match. No tunable caps. | AD-13 rationale, AD-28, AD-14, AD-32, AD-34, AD-40 |

Not decisions of this spec (do not fold in): the prompt and schema v2 story (multi-date rows, `locationContext`), the geocoding fix, recovery of already-stored posts.
