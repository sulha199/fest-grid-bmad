Coauthor attribution and subscription surfaces

The finding survived pressure-testing as a cross-layer change, but only after separating scraper identity from content identity and refusing historical ownership guesses.

Locked decisions

  • Normalized post-account roles with lossless legacy migration.
  • Verified identities are subscribable from context; provisional identities are internal-only.
  • Filtering includes publisher, coauthor, and scraping-source associations.
  • Event detail gets posted-at and coauthors; shared account info gets a confirmed accessible toggle.
  • Toggle analytics report sanitized success and failure; the future cap belongs to IDEA-008.

Rejected or deferred

  • No producer-array-order heuristic.
  • No automatic public account-directory population from every scraped identity.
  • No cap number chosen here; the PRD “2” and IDEA-008 “5” conflict.
  • Apify role semantics remain a prerequisite, not an assumption based on the Bright Data fixture.
