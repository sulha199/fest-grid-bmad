# Story 0.54: Amend EventCategory cardinality (safety cap 6, non-exclusive OTHER) and add SCIENCE_AND_ENGINEERING

## Story

As a developer maintaining FestGrid's AI event-extraction pipeline,
I want to replace `EventCategory`'s cap-4/`OTHER`-exclusive cardinality rule with a safety cap of 6 real categories and non-exclusive `OTHER`, add a 23rd category (`SCIENCE_AND_ENGINEERING`), and rewrite the UMKM/entrepreneurship, sports-branded-gear, and wedding-services-expo boundary rules accordingly,
so that the taxonomy stops collapsing genuinely multi-subject posts into `OTHER` or losing stated subjects to a too-low cap, engineering/research/maker content has a home instead of scattering across `EDUCATION`/`BUSINESS_AND_NETWORKING`/`OTHER`, and the golden corpus matches what AD-44's amended rules actually say (closing out backlog finding FIND-098).
